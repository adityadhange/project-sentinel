### Petition B: The Biohazard & Stranded Asset Case

- **Target:** Enforcing Water Act compliance, prosecuting the Municipal Commissioner under Sections 41/44, operationalizing the stranded 500 km sewerage network, levying Environmental Damage Compensation (EDC) via MPCB, securing city-wide injunctions/restitutions, and site-specific restoration for Majrewadi.
- **Standing & Parties:** Co-Applicants Netaji Shekumber Bhopale (aggrieved landholder, Survey Nos. 76 & 77, Majrewadi / Nala No. 1) and Civic Advocate Aditya V. Dhange. Responding entities include SMC, MPCB, and executing co-respondents Envirosafe Consultant and Shradha Construction.
- **Supporting Evidence Dossier & Architecture:**
  - *Report 3 & Relief Scope:* Illegal Sewage Conveyance & Stranded AMRUT Assets. Covers the aggregated 500 km sewerage network (~250 km Central AMRUT Mission by Das Offshore Engineering and ~250 km State schemes including Maharashtra Suvarna Jayanti Nagarotthan Maha-Abhiyan), uncommissioned with missing STP outfalls (40–50 MLD raw sewage outfalls). City-wide relief architecture includes interim ex-parte injunction across all 74 nalas, MPCB water testing across all outfalls, mandatory 100% STP interception within 90 days, forensic engineering audit of the 500 km asset by IIT Bombay/NEERI, Water Act Section 41/44 criminal prosecution, and site-specific restitution for Majrewadi.
