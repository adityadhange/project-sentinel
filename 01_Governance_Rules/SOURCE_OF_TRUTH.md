### 1. Fundamental Principle

Project Sentinel must operate from an explicitly governed and traceable body of authoritative information.

A document does not become authoritative merely because:
- It is newly uploaded.
- It has a higher version number.
- It is stored in GitHub.
- It was generated by an AI system.
- It is described as final, approved or canonical within its own contents.

Authority must be established through an explicit approval process.

### 2. Document Status Classification

Every document entering the repository must have an identifiable status.

The controlled status taxonomy is:

- **RATIFIED:** Formally adopted by an explicit Principal Operator ratification disposition, recorded with scope and traceability; binding within that approved scope.
- **APPROVED:** Formally accepted by the authorised operator; binding within the approved scope.
- **UNDER REVIEW:** Being examined; not yet authoritative.
- **CANDIDATE:** Proposed for adoption or integration.
- **HISTORICAL:** Retained for reference; not active authority.
- **SUPERSEDED:** Replaced by an explicitly identified approved document.
- **UNVERIFIED:** Claims or contents have not received the required verification.

No AI system may independently change a document's authority status.

### 3. Authority of Sources

The root `GEMINI.md` is the repository's **root source-precedence anchor**. Its Section 7, **Conflict and precedence**, is the controlling index for resolving instruction and record conflicts. This GOV-001 policy is the applicable living-governance layer identified at Level 2 of that hierarchy; it does not displace the hierarchy itself.

The approved precedence order is:

0. **Root authority anchor — `GEMINI.md` (ROOT-GEMINI-001, Version 4.0.0, RATIFIED).** It defines and controls this precedence hierarchy.
1. **Explicit current Principal Operator instruction**, within applicable safety and platform limits.
2. **Binding living governance policy** in `01_Governance_Rules/`, limited to documents individually marked `RATIFIED` or `APPROVED`; drafts and proposals are non-authoritative.
3. **Ratified Kernel v4.0 invariants and canonical specifications** in `00_Control_Plane_v4.0/`.
4. **Task-specific instructions and artifacts** within the authorized task pack, subordinate to Levels 0–3.
5. **Historical or archived material** in `02_Archive_Legacy/`, which is context only and never overrides active authority.

Where two active authoritative sources conflict, do not silently choose, merge, or synthesize a new rule. Identify the exact conflicting provisions and source locations, preserve both references, halt the affected action, and request the required Principal Operator governance disposition. Historical or archived material cannot override an active source merely because it is older, newer, more detailed, or described as canonical.

Any future change to the hierarchy requires an explicit Principal Operator decision and a controlled revision of `GEMINI.md` and this policy.

### 4. AI System Restrictions

ChatGPT, GitHub Copilot, Gemini and other AI systems may:

- Analyse documents.
- Identify inconsistencies.
- Suggest corrections.
- Propose consolidation.
- Identify missing information and dependencies.
- Prepare revised drafts.

They may not independently:

- Declare a document authoritative.
- Invent missing approvals or evidence.
- Resolve disputed architectural decisions without authorisation.
- Delete historical records.
- Change governance rules or mechanism identities without approval.
- Treat their own output as independent verification.

### 5. Preservation During Migration

During initial repository construction:

- Preserve original source documents.
- Do not discard duplicates before reconciliation.
- Distinguish original documents from reconstructed documents.
- Record known provenance and version information.
- Maintain a record of material changes.
- Do not assume that a reconstructed document is more authoritative than its source.

### 6. Approval Principle

The operator retains final authority over the adoption of architecture, governance rules, mechanisms, personas, skills and runtime instructions.

AI recommendations are advisory until explicitly approved.

GitHub storage, upload, editing or revision history does not itself constitute architectural approval.

### 7. Current Authority

This document is ratified as part of the Kernel v4.0 governance baseline. It establishes the living-governance rules for document status, source conflicts, preservation, and operator approval, subject to the root precedence anchor in `GEMINI.md`.

Ratification does not establish that every repository document is approved, that every historical claim is verified, or that host-platform permissions and enforcement exist. Each document retains its own recorded status, and capability claims must be grounded in actual host evidence.
